# Supplementary analyses

This page lists only analyses retained in the final manuscript and SI.

## Dual-view controls

The five-run MAEs are: Original only `25.278 ± 0.054 s`,
tautomer-standardized only `25.217 ± 0.070 s`, O+O `25.110 ± 0.041 s`, T+T
`25.056 ± 0.037 s`, same-seed O+T arithmetic mean `25.059 ± 0.038 s`, and final
O+T OOF Huber `25.055 ± 0.039 s`. Independent-model fusion is the major
contributor; standardization makes a smaller representation-robustness
contribution. It is not a model of solution-phase, pH-specific, or ESI tautomer
populations.

## Structural controls and ring subgroups

Five-run MAEs are `25.055 ± 0.039 s` for Full TC-TopoRT, `25.121 ± 0.091 s`
without explicit ring 2-cells, and `25.701 ± 0.069 s` for parameter-matched
atom-bond GINE. No2Cell retains conventional atom, bond, and global ring
descriptors. The acyclic, ring-containing, aromatic-ring, heterocycle, and
multi-ring subgroups generated by `analyze_ring_subgroups.py` overlap; their
counts must not be summed as mutually exclusive categories.

## Early-RT scope

The complete public SMRT source has 80,038 records. The `RT < 300 s` operational
subset contains 2,058 records (2.57%). The paper describes these as
early-eluting/non-retained under the SMRT RPLC method, not universally as highly
polar metabolites, and reports no predictive benchmark on the excluded subset.

## Tautomer-standardization audit

Strict standardization changes 37,724/70,182 training representations (53.75%)
and 4,242/7,798 test representations (54.40%). The final collision audit finds
77,973 distinct standardized structures, seven collision groups involving 14
records, and three groups spanning train and test. Collision examples are
representation-collision cases, not experimentally established separated
tautomer populations.

## Matched RIKEN Full-versus-No2Cell analysis

The target-domain metrics are:

| Model | MAE | RMSE | MedAE | Bias |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Full TC-TopoRT | 20.272 | 28.355 | 11.207 | 4.274 |
| w/o explicit ring 2-cells | 21.977 | 29.144 | 16.297 | 5.012 |

The analysis scope contains 85 independent target queries, 5,044 candidate rows,
4,692 unique structures, and true-candidate coverage for all 85 queries. There
are 84 structurally distinct same-formula query sets and 81 with at least one
ring-signature-different false candidate. Strict controlled stereoisomer pairs
are unavailable (`0`), so the analysis does not support stereoisomer-resolution
claims.

Query-clustered pairwise results are:

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| All same-formula | 84 | 4,888 | 80.64% | 77.22% | +3.42 pp | -0.74 to +7.68 |
| Ring-different | 81 | 2,844 | 82.28% | 81.07% | +1.21 pp | -3.81 to +5.95 |
| Original-MS Top-10 | 84 | 768 | 76.95% | 73.08% | +3.87 pp | -0.93 to +8.70 |
| Ring-different MS Top-10 | 72 | 386 | 77.67% | 77.11% | +0.56 pp | -7.04 to +8.08 |

The Full model is numerically higher, but uncertainty remains and every listed
interval spans zero. Norharman/beta-Carboline is an illustrative positive case,
not evidence of universal isomer resolution. The general pairwise analysis is
reproduced by `scripts/analysis/analyze_full_vs_no2cell_pairwise.py` when the
corresponding matched candidate input is supplied.
